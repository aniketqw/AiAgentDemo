Product Title: GDA CropID map for 2024 South Asia (250-meters)
Produced By: GDA Corp (GeoSpatial Data Analysis Corporation)
Released: May 1, 2024
Referencing the File: GDA CropID GIS map shall be referred to, in all instances, as "GDA_CropID_SouthAsia_2024_RapeseedWinter_250m"
Abstract: GDA CropID 2024 South Asia map is a GIS raster format product with 250-meter spatial resolution. The map offers 2024 winter (rabi) rapeseed locations for the top four rapeseed-producing provinces of India (Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Haryana), along with all of Bangladesh.
Coverage: Top four major rapeseed-producing provinces in India (Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Madhya Pradesh, and Haryana) and full coverage of Bangladesh.
Spatial Resolution: 250-meters. The original classification is performed at 10-meters.
File Format: GeoTIFF
Projection: ESPG:3857, WGS 84 / Pseudo-Mercator.
Crop Harvest Year: 2024
Classification Categories: ID  NAME
96 Rape Seed / Canola (Winter / Rabi)
Crop Area Estimates: GDA 2024 South Asian winter/rabi rapeseed area estimates are available in CropSignal™ under the "GDA Area" source.
